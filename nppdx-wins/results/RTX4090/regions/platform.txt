Workload (nppdx-wins W1, smoke test): 3840x2160 NV12 frame (seed 1), NV12->RGB BT.601 limited range, box 5x5 over the frame (the mean, per channel), N in {1, 16, 100} regions of 32x16 per iteration at Experiment 1's positions (BENCH_REGIONS=32x16), each resized 1:2 (bilinear) to 64x32, then Mul (1/255), Sub (0.485, 0.456, 0.406), Div (0.229, 0.224, 0.225), float3 tensor of N planes 64x32.
Semantics (documented, not matched): FKL samples the resize at dst * 0.5 and clamps at the crop (box over the frame, crop, resize); NPPDx samples at pixel centers and its frame mode gives the resize the boxed frame around the region. Each is checked against a reference of its own semantics.
Regions: iteration t of every loop (the 3 warm-up iterations are t in [0, 3), the 10 timed ones t in [3, 13)) runs every pipeline on the regions of iteration t, generated before the loops and the same for every implementation, program and machine (statistics of the timed ones in regions.csv). Each pipeline converts them into its library's arguments before the loops; inside the timed region is what the library needs to receive the regions of the iteration: FKL's build() calls and the fusion of the chain, and the per-region arguments and calls of the baselines (see their pipelines).
Timing: 3 warm-up then 10 timed iterations. In every iteration nppdx, fkl_inline, then FKL staged run one after the other, each timed with CUDA events on one stream around the complete pipeline (all host enqueue work included) and followed by a synchronization. FKL staged's speedup over each baseline is computed per iteration (baseline time / staged time) and averaged (mean speedup, FKL's two-execution convention). Median, p10, p90 with linear interpolation.
GPU time only: a second pass with the same interleaved iterations and regions and CUPTI kernel tracing on; the GPU time of one execution is the sum of the execution times of its kernels (no host work, no launch gaps, no memory copies), and FKL staged's speedups are again computed per execution and averaged.
Validation: before the timing, every pipeline on the regions of the first, every 50th and the last timed iteration, against its reference (validation.csv, one row per iteration): 1e-3 for FKL staged against the float reference, one level of 8 bit RGB for the 8 bit pipelines against theirs.
Kernel counts: CUPTI activity tracing of one pipeline (regions of the first timed iteration), in a separate pass after all timings
